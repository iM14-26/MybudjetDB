Verification

Credit = Opening + Deposited − Withdrawn − Fees (All) + Cash back (all).
Fees = rows with Details "Fees"; Fees (Withdrawn records) = the Fees column; Fees (All) = both.
Cash back (P2:P) = the whole of column P, shown on Ahli Credit2; Cash back (Deposited records) = rows with Details "Cash Back"; Cash back (all) = both.
Opening balances are saved with the web app.

Compare cash back with a column from your sheet

In the sheet, select your cash back column (for example P2 down to the last row), copy it and paste it below. Enter the sheet row where the paste starts. The dashboard then lists the rows whose cash back differs.
